Joseph Murphy Brisendine

I trained in philosophy and then in biophysics. My research asked how proteins store, move, and compute with information: de novo protein design, charge transport through peptides, and engineered allosteric switches, carried out in Ronald Koder's lab at the City College of New York. I left academia in 2021 and spent the next four years teaching high school math.

In 2026 I saw what agentic AI made possible and went back to the questions I had felt closest to answering when I left. I couldn't finish them on my own with what was available to me then. Everything I've done since has been a collaboration with Claude (Anthropic), and I present it as exactly that: an example of using AI for good.
