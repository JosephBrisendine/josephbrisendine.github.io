Membrane signaling explorer

Gaussian mode model of a near-critical, lipid-conserving membrane. How many bits a signaling motif gets per signal at a 1 kT sender budget. A model built for intuition, not a measurement.

What's in the model

A Gaussian (Ornstein–Zernike) composition field on a lattice (0.8 nm spacing) with conserved lipid diffusion (D ≈ 1 µm²/s). The receptor ("push") is a disk that switches its preference for raft-like lipids. The reader is a ring of patches at 3× the push radius. Efficiency comes from linear response and is turned into bits per window for a binary signal at a fixed 1 kT dissipation.

Corrals are periodic boxes. Leaky corrals let the corral-average composition exchange with neighbours on a 10 ms hop time; sealed ones freeze it. "Open" is a 410 nm box with no exchange.

Not included: mobile partners and contact readouts, active (actin-driven) clustering, nonlinear strong switching, and receiver-side costs.
